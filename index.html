    A bit about me
    
      
    I'm objmsu11, a Roblox developer who works on both the visual side and the code side of a game. I model in Blender and Studio, and I write everything in Luau.
    
      
    Doing both means I can take a feature from an idea to something that works in your game: the model, the script that drives it and how players interact with it.
    
      
    
        Right now
        I'm overhauling my own disaster-survival game, Don't Fall, with a new lobby, new maps, coins, a shop and progression.
