Menu music

Original music made for this game and played live by your browser (no audio files). Every mode has its own track: Quiz Show (Classic), Last Second (Survival, speeding up as the clock runs down), Neural Arena (Human vs AI), Showtime (Category Game), Hidden Door (Riddle Mode) and Final Riddle (boss riddles). Pick the menu music in the Music panel (🎵 at the top of the home screen); it never replaces a mode’s own soundtrack. Games end with a short victory or defeat tune. Sound effects work with the music on or off.
